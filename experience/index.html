Northrop Grumman

Software Engineer Intern, then Computer Vision & Perception Systems Intern

Embedded software · C · Embedded systems · CAD

May – August 2025

I started as a software engineer intern focused on autonomous drones and land-mine detection, then moved to computer vision and perception systems, focused on sensors and advanced technology. My mechanical design work there included the trash-collection mechanism for a water-wheel trash interceptor in the Chesapeake Bay.

More detail

For the interceptor I produced the CAD models and drawings for the rake, conveyor, shafts, bearings and supports, sized the drive torque to cut jams, and analyzed wheel output against pumping power at low flow with marine and structural engineers. For a solar-powered water-quality platform, I designed sensor mounts and sealed enclosures for pH, dissolved oxygen, turbidity and temperature sensors, and supported the boat and pier trials.
