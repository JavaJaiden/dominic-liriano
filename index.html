I study mechanical engineering at the University of Maryland. I design the mechanical side of autonomous systems: mounts, enclosures, airframes and mechanisms, taken from CAD and drawings to prototypes and field trials.

I’m a first-generation college student. I was raised in Maryland by a single mother who became the youngest and first female captain in the DC Fire Department, and my father immigrated from the Dominican Republic and became a government contractor. I grew up balancing athletics, including Division 1 baseball, with construction and security work, and I serve as a firefighter with DC Fire and EMS.

At Northrop Grumman, I worked on autonomous drones and land-mine detection and then on sensors, and designed the trash-collection mechanism for a water-wheel trash interceptor in the Chesapeake Bay. This summer at M.C. Dean, I built a Python tool that turns Excel inputs into AutoCAD fire-alarm riser diagrams, cutting drawing time by 90%, and assembled and tested 50+ VESDA panels.

I’m president of the Northrop Grumman Innovation Lab, a student-led defense-tech lab with 250+ students, and a board member of the Society of Hispanic Professional Engineers. I co-founded narb with my best friends, and in October 2026 I represented xFoundry at the launch of the Clark School’s Idea Factory, talking about building NoTestudo.

I’m president of the Northrop Grumman Innovation Lab. The best way to reach me is LinkedIn or email.
